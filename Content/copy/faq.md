# FAQ

## What is a NAWI?
A Non-Automatic Weighing Instrument is a weighing instrument that requires the intervention of an operator during the weighing process.

## What is OIML R76?
OIML R76 is an international recommendation covering non-automatic weighing instruments, including metrological and technical requirements and associated tests.

## Is this platform a replacement for legal verification?
No. The platform is a digital workflow and record-keeping tool. Applicable legal verification, approval, certification, and enforcement remain governed by the competent authority and applicable regulations.

## Can test results be edited?
A production implementation should use role-based permissions and an audit trail for any amendment to a finalized record.
