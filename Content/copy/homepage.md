# Precision. Compliance. Confidence.

## Hero
A digital testing and compliance platform for Non-Automatic Weighing Instruments (NAWIs).

Test instruments, record measurements, evaluate results, and generate structured compliance reports through a clear digital workflow.

## Core capabilities
- Guided instrument testing
- OIML R76-oriented test workflows
- Measurement and error recording
- Compliance status tracking
- Evidence and result traceability
- Report-ready test records
