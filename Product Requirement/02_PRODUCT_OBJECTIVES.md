# Product Objectives

1. Digitize the NAWI evaluation workflow.
2. Centralize instrument/evaluation data.
3. Provide structured digital test forms.
4. Automate configured calculations.
5. Evaluate compliance using versioned rules.
6. Validate observations and inputs.
7. Explain calculation results.
8. Generate standardized reports.
9. Preserve instrument/evaluation history.
10. Provide secure RBAC.
11. Maintain audit trails.
12. Support future ruleset versions.

Experience goals:
- premium interactive 3D public website
- scroll-driven storytelling
- dark/light modes
- clear transition from physical instrument to digital evaluation
- usable professional application underneath
